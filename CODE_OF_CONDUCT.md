# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

To promote a respectful, inclusive, and open environment, we as contributors and maintainers commit to ensuring that participation in the Omnex project and community remains a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, gender identity or expression, experience level, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual orientation and identity.

## Our Standards

Examples of positive behavior that help create a welcoming and supportive community include:

* Using kind and inclusive language  
* Respecting different perspectives and experiences  
* Accepting constructive feedback gracefully  
* Prioritizing the community’s well-being  
* Demonstrating empathy and respect toward others  

Examples of unacceptable behavior include:

* The use of sexualized language or imagery, and any unwelcome sexual attention or advances  
* Trolling, insulting, or derogatory comments, as well as personal or political attacks  
* Public or private harassment  
* Sharing others’ private information (e.g., physical or email addresses) without consent  
* Any conduct that could reasonably be considered unprofessional or inappropriate  

## Our Responsibilities

Project maintainers are responsible for clearly communicating standards of acceptable behavior and are expected to respond fairly and appropriately to any violations.

They have the right and authority to remove, edit, or reject comments, commits, code, documentation edits, issues, and other contributions that violate this Code of Conduct. Maintainers may also temporarily or permanently ban contributors whose behavior is deemed inappropriate, threatening, or harmful.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies both within project spaces and in public spaces where an individual represents the Omnex project or community. Examples include using an official project email address, posting via an official social media account, or representing the project at an event. Project maintainers may define additional representation guidelines as needed.

## Enforcement

Instances of abusive, harassing, or otherwise unacceptable behavior can be reported to the Omnex project team at [info@omnex.io](mailto:info@omnex.io). All reports will be reviewed, investigated, and addressed as appropriate to the situation. The project team is committed to maintaining confidentiality regarding the reporter of an incident. Additional enforcement procedures may be published separately if needed.

Maintainers who fail to uphold this Code of Conduct in good faith may face temporary or permanent consequences as determined by the project leadership.
